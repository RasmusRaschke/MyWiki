```ad-Definition
title: Definition (Isomorphism).
Let $\mathtt{C}$ be a category. An <u>isomorphism</u> is a morphism $f: a \to b$ such that there exists another morphism $g: b \to a$ with $fg=1_a$ and $gf=1_b$. Then, we call $a$ and $b$ <u>isomorphic</u> and write $a \cong b$. An [[category|endomorphism]] which is also an isomorphism is called <u>automorphism</u>.
```

_Examples_

1. In $\Set$, isomorphisms are bijective maps.
2. In $\Top$, isomorphisms are bijective, continuous maps with continuous inverse. Similarly, in $\Man$ they are bijective, smooth maps with smooth inverse.
3. In $\Grp$, $\Ring$, $\Vec$ and $\Mod$, they are bijective homomorphisms.
