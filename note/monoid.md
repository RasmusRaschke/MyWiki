```ad-Definition
title: Definition (Monoid).
A <u>monoid</u> is a pair $(M, \cdot)$ consisting of a [[set|set]] $M$ and a binary operation $$\cdot: M \times M \to M$$ such that the following holds:
- $\forall a,b,c \in M: \, (a \cdot b) \cdot c = a \cdot (b \cdot c)$
- $\exists e \in M: \, \forall a \in M: \, e \cdot a = a \cdot e = a$
```
