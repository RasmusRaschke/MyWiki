```ad-Definition
title: Definition (Universal Property).
Let $\mathtt{C}$ be a [[category|category]] and $c \in \ob \mathtt{C}$. A <u>universal property</u> of $c$ is a [[functor|representable functor]] $\mathcal{F}$ together with a <u>universal element</u> $x \in \ob \mathcal{F}c$ that defines the [[natural_transformation|natural isomorphism]] $\mathtt{C}(c,-) \cong \mathcal{F}$ or $\mathtt{C}(-,c)\cong \mathcal{F}$, as appropriate, via the [[yoneda|yoneda lemma]].
```
